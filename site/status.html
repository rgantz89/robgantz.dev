How it works

All of it is defined in Terraform and deployed through GitHub Actions.

EventBridgeTriggers the check every 5 minutes.
LambdaRequests each page, reads the certificate, emits CloudWatch metrics.
DynamoDBKeeps daily results for 30 days to compute uptime and the error budget.
CloudWatch + SNSAlarms email me on downtime, missing headers, or an expiring certificate.
